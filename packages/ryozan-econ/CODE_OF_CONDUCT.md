# Code of Conduct

## The standard

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to get something right.

Concretely: no harassment, no personal attacks, no demeaning comments about who
someone is. Disagree with the argument, not the person making it. Accept that a
maintainer may say no to a change and still be acting in good faith.

## Scope

Applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space, and to
conduct elsewhere that would reasonably make a contributor unsafe here.

## Enforcement

Report a problem privately to the maintainers through GitHub. Reports are handled
confidentially. Responses range from a private word to a permanent ban, chosen by
the maintainers according to severity and pattern.

Maintainers who do not hold themselves to this are subject to it as well.
